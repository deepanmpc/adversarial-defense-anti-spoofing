Why This Is Architecturally Different

Most fraud systems evaluate claims in isolation and ask: "Is this claim suspicious?" This system models the entire claims network in real time and asks: "Is this claim consistent — across physics, behavior, history, and network topology — with a human being in genuine distress?" The distinction is not cosmetic. A single fraudulent claim looks like noise. Five hundred synchronized fraudulent claims from the same toolkit, triggered by the same Telegram alert — that's a graph signature. This system is built to see it.
